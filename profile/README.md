## About Netwide Network

**Netwide Network** is an independent development studio based in Sweden.

We create across software, web, games and virtual environments — from focused websites, applications and utilities to backend systems, integrations, game systems, scripts, custom maps, interiors and digital assets.

Our work is influenced by a Swedish and broader Nordic design mindset: clean presentation, practical solutions, restrained visual design and attention to the details that make a product feel complete.

We deliberately do not define Netwide by a single platform, framework or product category.

Different ideas deserve different tools.

What connects our work is the standard behind it: thoughtful design, solid engineering, useful functionality and the willingness to keep improving after the first version ships.

Some projects are public, some remain private, and others begin as experiments. This GitHub organization is where the work that is ready to be shared lives.

## Virtual Worlds & Environments

Software does not always stop at the screen.

Part of Netwide Network's work focuses on building the environments in which digital experiences take place.

Our current environment development is primarily focused around **GTA V and FiveM**, including custom interiors, map development and world modification.

This includes:

- **MLOs & Interiors** — custom buildings, interiors and purpose-built spaces
- **YMAPs & World Editing** — map additions, object placement and environmental changes
- **Props & Assets** — custom environmental objects and reusable world assets
- **World Design** — creating environments that support both gameplay and atmosphere
- **Gameplay Integration** — designing spaces alongside the systems and scripts that use them

Our environment work often takes inspiration from **Swedish and Scandinavian architecture, infrastructure and public environments**.

Where appropriate, we aim to create spaces that feel grounded, functional and recognizably Nordic rather than generic.

The goal is the same whether we are writing software or building a physical environment inside a virtual world:

> **Build something that feels intentional.**

## A Nordic Perspective

Being based in Sweden influences more than where we work.

The Nordic approach to design often values simplicity, function, clarity and restraint — qualities that align closely with how we want Netwide projects to feel.

That does not mean every project has to look Scandinavian.

It means we value thoughtful interfaces over unnecessary complexity, practical solutions over noise and environments that feel believable rather than overdesigned.

Whether we are building software, a website, a game system or a virtual environment, the goal is to create something that feels deliberate from the first interaction to the smallest detail.

## Projects & Public Work

This organization is intentionally curated.

We would rather publish fewer repositories that are genuinely useful and maintainable than fill the profile with unfinished work simply for the sake of having more public projects.

A project may remain private while it is experimental, commercial, internal or simply not ready to represent the standard we want attached to the Netwide name.

When something is ready to be shared, it belongs here.

Public projects may include software, applications, tools, libraries, scripts, game systems, development resources and other work created under the Netwide Network identity.

Where a project is open source, its repository will define its own license, documentation, contribution guidelines and support expectations.

<br>

## What We Value

**Quality over quantity.**  
We would rather build fewer things properly than release something simply because we can.

**Function with purpose.**  
Every feature, system and design choice should have a reason to exist.

**Clean foundations.**  
Maintainable code, sensible structure and clear architecture matter long after the first version works.

**Consistency.**  
The small details matter. A polished experience is the result of hundreds of decisions that feel like they belong together.

**Continuous improvement.**  
No project is ever truly finished. There is always something to refine, simplify, optimize or rethink.

**Exploration.**  
Netwide is not restricted to one technology, platform or industry. If an idea is worth exploring, we want the freedom to build it.
